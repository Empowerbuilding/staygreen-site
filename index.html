The Problem

Your Compliance Grade Is a Revenue Switch

Going red isn't a paperwork problem — it's lost work you never even see. And it's almost never about safety. It's admin failure: an expired cert, a missed quarterly report, a COI with the wrong wording.

Blocked 2AM Call-Outs

A field manager types your vendor ID into SAP at 2am. If your light is red, the PO auto-blocks and the next green vendor gets the call. That's a $2K–$50K job gone in one keystroke.

Training Qualifications

TQ Is the Biggest Admin Drag in the Business

Every operator has its own TQ matrix — SafeLand, H2S, fit tests, orientations, LOTO. Before any crew rolls out, someone has to verify every hand is green for that operator. StayGreen makes that check instant.

  • 4Hand #3 is red? Start over with the replacement — and the next one
