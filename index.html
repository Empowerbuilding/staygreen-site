The Problem

The Qualification Gap.

Generic Cards, Not Your Procedures

Third-party qualification programs test covered tasks — never your own O&M procedures. When an auditor asks whether Tuesday's crew worked to your lockout procedure, generic cards have nothing to say.

The Clipboard Proves Nothing

A paper JSA says the crew was briefed. It doesn't say which procedure revision governed the work, who read it, or whether anyone re-acknowledged after the last revision.

The 7 AM Spot Check

An auditor rolls up unannounced and asks for the JSA and the procedures it was worked to. One's in a truck three miles away, the other's a shrug. Your pad sits down waiting on paper.
